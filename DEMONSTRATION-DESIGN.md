# Approved demonstration-first direction

This supersedes the field-manual treatment in DESIGN.md. The owner approved a concept centred on captured lesson images, practice entry and compact rows. ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1; anti-slop applied during implementation.

- Actual reference frames give each lesson its identity. Missing references have an honest fallback. Generated concept photography is not shipped.
- Neutral white and cool off-white support comparing images and reading instructions. Muted teal marks actions and selected filters; existing warnings communicate actual conditions.
- Manrope headings and DM Sans instructions match the approved concept and keep control labels readable. Serif headings and decorative numbers are removed.
- Horizontal desktop navigation releases space for demonstrations. Mobile keeps every destination behind a labelled, keyboard-accessible menu. Its shadow marks the overlay; other surfaces stay flat.
- Unfinished practice earns the resume action; otherwise a published lesson offers an entry point. No invented progress percentage or recommendation claim.
- Search, task-source filters and publication filters operate on actual records. The review queue shows owned drafts and unresolved questions, disappearing when empty.
- Frame strips show saved references in their existing order. They do not pretend to be video playback.
- Feature image and text sit side by side on desktop and stack on mobile. Rows group thumbnail, title and status with dividers. The review queue moves below the library on smaller screens.
- Only media and editing groups need bounded surfaces. No decorative dashboard metrics, promotional banner or filler process panel.
- Interaction feedback respects reduced motion; no automatic decorative motion. Existing semantic action icons remain consistent across capture and review.
- A fixed light surface deliberately supports reference comparison and reading.

All capture, review, publication, consent, authentication, export and practice functions remain. The authenticated frame endpoint protects images; students receive published reference IDs, not private draft changes.
