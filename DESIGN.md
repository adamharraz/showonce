# ShowOnce visual direction

ShowOnce is a workspace for learning physical and screen-based tasks through reviewed demonstrations. The owner wants a professional, simple interface that feels interesting and engaging, without promotional banners, repeated slogans or generic AI decoration.

Reading: an editorial workshop for instructors and adult students. ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.

- Palette: warm paper, charcoal ink, muted teal. Paper suits sustained reading; ink gives instructions definition; teal identifies the primary action and selected controls.
- Typography: Georgia for page titles, DM Sans for controls and instructions. The serif gives lessons the character of a field manual; the sans keeps editing and practice easy to scan.
- Motif: numbered lesson entries and pronounced horizontal rules. Indexing helps locate lessons and mirrors the numbered instructions inside them.
- Library: a contents page, with compact counts and working filters. The next decision is which lesson to review or practice, rather than which dashboard metric to inspect.
- Navigation: keep existing destinations and responsive behaviour. Dark navigation separates the workbench controls from the light lesson reading surface.
- Spacing: generous around page titles, tighter around lesson entries and form controls. Reading and doing require different densities.
- Surfaces: flat reading areas and clearly bounded editing groups. Cards group editable content, rather than decorate the library.
- The single offset shadow on sign-in separates the entry form from the reading workspace; lesson and editor surfaces stay flat.
- Icons: retain camera, screen, phone, ordering and action symbols where they identify a control; remove sparkle symbols that imply AI magic.
- Status: published versions, unresolved questions, capture state and connection state remain visible because they affect the next action.
- Motion: short hover, pressed and selection transitions only. Respect reduced motion; no automatic floating or looping animation.
- Theme: a fixed light reading surface is deliberate for comparing captured evidence and reading long instructions. A dark reading mode is outside this revision.
- Content: actual lesson data and actual session records only. Test screenshots are explicitly labelled fixture evidence.

Preserve capture, phone pairing, authentication, consent, review, required clarification, immutable publishing, exports and practice feedback.
